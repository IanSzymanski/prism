# How Cedar Hollow got its *afternoons* back

Four months after rollout, caseworkers spend less time on notes and more time with families.
:::

Hi ${Contacts.First Name},

Cedar Hollow Family Services had a familiar problem: case notes piled up after every home visit.

![Caseworker reviewing a draft note.](prism:placeholder){.hero dark="prism:placeholder-dark"}

::: stats
- **41%** less time drafting case notes
- **Same day** median time to a finalized note
- **94%** of drafts approved after review
:::

## What *changed*

::: features
- []{.icon .ph-shield-check} **Human control** Staff review, correct and approve every draft.
- []{.icon .ph-clock} **Time back** Notes are finished the day of the visit.
:::
