Maple Ridge Youth Services got intake down from 5 days to 2 days, from referral to first visit scheduled.

Their intake packet was 23 pages, and case managers retyped each referral into 4 different forms. Smart forms now pull referral information into the intake forms, as configured for Maple Ridge's packet. CARA flags details that need attention, like upcoming court dates.

Case managers review every field before it is saved. Nothing goes into the record without a person checking it.

"My team used to spend Mondays typing. Now they spend Mondays calling kids back."

At Maple Ridge, missed court dates went from 3 to 0 in the quarter after go-live. CARA flagged court dates so they showed up for the case manager to act on. Case managers still reviewed, corrected and approved every intake, and decided what to do about each flag. AI output can miss things or get them wrong, so review is part of how the work gets done.
