## General AI tools don't know *casework*

Chat tools can write a paragraph. They don't know the case, the program's rules, or who has to sign off. Case Amplify is built around the record, so every draft starts from what the team already knows.

### From conversation to record

1. **Talk** with the client as usual.
2. **Capture** the conversation.
3. **Draft** a note from it.
4. **Review** the draft against the case.
5. **Correct** what needs changing.
6. **Approve** before anything is saved.
7. **Follow up** with reminders and messages.

### Built for human services

For teams that carry caseloads and document every contact, where accuracy and accountability matter as much as speed.

## One record for the whole case, with people in\ *charge*

::: features
- []{.icon .ph-folder-user .accent} **Connected case record** History, contacts, documents and notes together.
- []{.icon .ph-chat-circle-text .accent} **AI-assisted documentation** Draft notes from conversations, ready for review.
- []{.icon .ph-clipboard-text .accent} **Smart forms** Fill known details in forms set up for your program.
- []{.icon .ph-bell-simple .accent} **Case monitoring** See deadlines and cases that need attention.
- []{.icon .ph-device-mobile .accent} **SMS communication** Text clients from the case, with the thread on record.
- []{.icon .ph-chart-bar .accent} **Reporting** Program reports from the same record.
:::

::: band
### CARA drafts, your staff decide

CARA, the Case Amplify assistant, prepares the draft. Nothing is saved until a person approves it, and forms, fields and workflows are configured to how your program works.

::: flow
- **Review** Read the draft next to the case.
- **Correct** Edit anything that isn't right.
- **Approve** Only approved text is saved.
