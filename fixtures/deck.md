## Meet *Maple Ridge*

Maple Ridge Youth Services is a fictional agency.

- Foster care and independent living programs
- Young people aged 16 to 21
- About 40 case managers

::: notes
The agency is an example, not a real customer. It helps to picture a mid-sized youth services team.
:::
::::

:::: {.slide .stats}
## Where intake got *stuck*

- **23 pages** in the intake packet
- **Four forms** retyped from each referral
- **5 days** on average from referral to first visit scheduled

::: notes
Intake was the big problem. Case managers were retyping information from the referral into four different forms. Details also got lost: a referral would say a young person had a court date, and nobody saw it.
:::
::::

:::: {.slide .media}
## What they *changed*

![Placeholder image: a case manager on a home visit (fictional).](prism:placeholder){.fade}

### Rolled out in March

- Smart Forms pull referral information into the intake forms.
- Case managers review everything before it is saved.
- CARA flags details like court dates so the team can act on them.

::: notes
Maple Ridge used Case Amplify Smart Forms. People stay in charge: case managers review everything before it is saved, and CARA's flags are for the team to act on.
:::

## Intake down to *2 days*

```chart
type: bar
caption: Days to first visit by month, Maple Ridge. Fictional data.
highlight: Mar-Jun
marker: Mar | Rollout
Jan: 5.2
Feb: 4.9
Mar: 3.1
Apr: 2.4
May: 2.1
Jun: 2.0
```

After three months, intake went from 5 days to 2 days.

::: notes
Rollout was in March. Days to first visit fell from 5.2 in January to 2.0 in June.
