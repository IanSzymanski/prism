## At a glance

- **38%** less time from first call to first visit
- **12 minutes** saved on each intake form
- **4 of 5** staff would keep the new process

## The problem

Maple Street's front desk retyped every intake form into two systems. New patients waited nine days for a first visit, and most of that time went to paperwork.

## What changed

The clinic moved intake to one shared form that fills both systems. Staff check the form instead of retyping it.

- One form, filled once
- A daily review of new forms at 9 a.m.
- A named owner for every open form

## The result

> We stopped chasing paper and started calling patients back the same day.
>
> Jordan Lee, Front Desk Lead

## What's next

Maple Street plans to bring the same form to its second site this winter.
