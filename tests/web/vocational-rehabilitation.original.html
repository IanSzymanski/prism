Built for the work behind employment

A VR case is more than a case record.

It can include conversations, assessments, eligibility information, employment goals, plans, providers, training, accommodations, documents, services, job development, and months or years of follow-up.


Case Amplify keeps that work connected so counselors can spend less time reconstructing what happened and more time making the decisions that move employment goals forward.

From intake through closure

Support the full vocational rehabilitation lifecycle

Reduce administrative work at each stage while keeping counselors, consumers, services, information, and next steps connected.

Intake and Eligibility

Guide conversations, collect records, extract document data, complete configured forms, and identify missing information before it delays the case.

Planning and Services

Bring employment goals, accommodations, services, providers, responsibilities, and deadlines together. Cara, Case Amplify's AI assistant, can summarize recent activity and help staff identify information or next steps that may need attention.

Preparation and Job Search

Track training and service participation, collect progress updates, send reminders, request documents, and keep case notes current as employment preparation moves forward.

Placement and Retention

Use check-ins to collect employment updates, hours, wages, accommodations, and new barriers. Responses that may require counselor attention can be surfaced for follow-up.

Closure and Reporting

Prepare current summaries, closure documentation, structured outcome data, and reports using information gathered throughout the case.
