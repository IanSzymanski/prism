## Frequently Asked *Questions* {.left}

::: faq
### What is vocational rehabilitation case management software?

Vocational rehabilitation case management software helps agencies manage consumer records, eligibility, plans, services, documentation, communication, employment progress, closure, and reporting throughout the VR process.

### How does Case Amplify reduce documentation work?

Case Amplify can turn conversations and documents into reviewable note drafts, current case summaries, structured information, and configured form data.

**Counselors review the work instead of starting each item from a blank page.**

### What can Case Amplify automate?

Case Amplify can draft notes and summaries, extract information, help complete configured forms, create tasks, send reminders and check-ins, request documents, collect employment updates, identify missing information, and make approved data available for reporting.

### Can Case Amplify work with our current VR system?

**Yes.**

Case Amplify can serve as the primary platform for a program or work alongside systems an agency wants to keep.

The right approach depends on the desired scope, required integrations, and reporting needs.

### Can our agency configure its own forms and workflows?

**Yes.**

Agencies can configure case types, data definitions, forms, guided questions, programs, workflows, reports, roles, and permissions around their requirements.

### How does Case Amplify support RSA-911 reporting?

Case Amplify supports configurable disability, service, and SOC-related data structures along with prebuilt or configurable dashboards, reports, queries, and structured exports.

**Agency-specific validation and submission requirements are confirmed during implementation.**

### Can counselors verify AI-generated information?

**Yes.**

Counselors review and approve generated notes, extracted information, and suggested actions.

Extracted values can include citations linking them to the relevant conversation or document.

### How can consumers provide updates and documents?

Consumers can respond to questions and check-ins, receive reminders, and securely provide requested documents through familiar, password-less interactions such as SMS-linked requests.

**They do not need to install another app.**

### How is sensitive consumer information protected?

Case Amplify uses role-based access, audit logs, data-access logs, and tenant-level controls.

**Consumer data is not used to train public AI models.**
:::
