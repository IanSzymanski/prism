Reducing burnout in human services

Burnout in social services rarely starts with the people. It starts with the paperwork, the re-entry, and the follow-ups that pile up between conversations.

Case Amplify is an AI-powered case management system that turns everyday conversations into actionable case information, so your team spends less time reconstructing context and more time supporting clients.

Burnout is a workload problem

Caseworkers choose this work to help people. Too much of the day goes somewhere else.

Notes written after hours from memory. The same details typed into three systems. Case history scattered across email, texts, and spreadsheets. Each task is small. Together they take the time and attention that belong to the people being served.

• Turning conversations into formal case notes
• Re-entering the same information in several places
• Searching long histories for the detail that matters
• Tracking follow-ups and deadlines by hand

Take the weight off each part of the day

After every conversation

Notes that start drafted

Case Amplify transcribes the conversation, drafts the case note, and pulls out structured information, each linked to the moment it came from.

Caseworkers review, correct, and approve. No more rebuilding a meeting from memory at the end of the day.

• Draft case notes and summaries from conversations
• Extract structured data with source citations
• Help complete configured forms from what was already said
• Keep the caseworker in control of what reaches the record

Before every meeting

Walk in with the full picture

CARA, the Case Amplify assistant, summarizes case history and surfaces what changed since the last contact.

Staff spend less time searching and more time listening, with updates from the rest of the team already in view.

• Summarize long case histories
• See changes recorded by other staff
• Spot missing information before it causes delays
• Ask questions grounded in authorized case information

Between appointments

Follow-up that doesn't live in your head

Workflows run your process in the background: reminders, check-ins, information requests, and surfacing cases that need a person.

Authorized users describe the process in plain language, and Case Amplify turns it into rules. Clients respond by text message, with no app to download.

• Send reminders and check-ins automatically
• Request documents and updates by text
• Surface priority messages for review
• Flag cases where follow-up has slowed

For supervisors

Support your team before they're stretched thin

Case IQ builds dashboards from a plain-language request, so managers can see caseload conditions without assembling reports.

Spot uneven workloads, stalled follow-ups, and documentation gaps early, and coach from the same reviewed case data your team works in.

• Ask for the view you need in plain language
• See caseload and follow-up status across staff
• Find documentation gaps before they become deadlines
• Keep dashboards private or share them across the organization
