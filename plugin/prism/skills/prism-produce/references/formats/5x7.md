# 5x7 card (postcard, invitation, mailer, handout; PDF, two pages at most)

Build: `run.sh sheet formats/5x7.md out/<tag>.pdf` (tag: see Naming in SKILL.md). The `layout: 5x7` line switches the sheet builder to a 5 × 7 in card at true size. Page 1 is the front, page 2 the back (optional). **Two pages at most:** a third side stops the build. Print double-sided, flip on the long edge (portrait) or short edge (landscape). Also read components.md.

## Front matter

```yaml
---
pagetitle: Harbor Point open house card   # plain text: PDF title
layout: 5x7
orientation: landscape                    # optional: 7 × 5 in. Default portrait (5 × 7 in)
bleed: true                               # optional: adds 1/8 in on every edge for a commercial printer
---
```

No masthead, title area or footer: the front side carries them.

## Sides

Each side is one `::::` block. Use `::::` so components inside keep `:::`.

| Side | Block | Layouts (pick one class) |
|---|---|---|
| Front (page 1) | `:::: {.side .front}` | standard (no class), `.cover`, `.statement` |
| Back (page 2, optional) | `:::: {.side .back}` | standard (no class), `.split`, `.mailer` |

Add `.dark` to a side for the brand's dark ground (at most one dark side per card). Pick the layouts that fit the piece, and offer the others in one line. A file with no side blocks becomes a one-sided card (its whole body is the front).

### Front layouts

**Standard:** logo, eyebrow, title and lede from the top, then a short block or two (stats, a QR code). `::: push` pins a block to the bottom edge.

```markdown
:::: {.side .front}
![](prism:logo){.logo}

[Open house · Saturday]{.eyebrow}

# See the new *clinic*

[One or two sentences.]{.lede}

::: {.stats .push}
- **12** new exam rooms
:::
::::
```

**Cover (`.cover`):** a photo edge to edge across the top 3.1 in (portrait) or down the left 3.3 in (landscape), the words under or beside it. Same blocks as the brochure cover:

```markdown
:::: {.side .front .cover}
::: hero-image
![](images/front.jpg){.fade}
:::

::: cover-body
![](prism:logo){.logo}

[Eyebrow]{.eyebrow}

# Title with an *accent* word

[One or two sentences.]{.lede}
:::
::::
```

Photo: landscape for portrait cards, portrait or square for landscape cards; it crops around its focal point. Keep the title to about 8 words.

**Statement (`.statement`):** one large line centred on the side, the lede under it, the logo pinned at the foot with `::: push`. For invitations, save-the-dates and announcements; best with `.dark`. Title at most about 6 words.

```markdown
:::: {.side .front .statement .dark}
[You're invited]{.eyebrow}

# Harbor Point, *ten years on*

[An evening with the people who built it · June 12]{.lede}

::: push
![](prism:logo-on-dark){.logo}
:::
::::
```

### Back layouts

**Standard:** eyebrow, `##` heading, a sentence or a short list, one component (checks, stats, features stack to one column), and a closing line. `::: push` pins the closing (a QR code, the contact line, a legal note) to the bottom.

**Split (`.split`):** a heading across the top, then two `::: col` side by side. Best in landscape. A `::: push` after the columns pins a line across the bottom.

**Mailer (`.mailer`):** the right half (landscape) or bottom 3 in (portrait) stays blank for the mailing address and postage, behind a hairline. Put the message on the other part; keep it short. An optional `::: indicia` block draws the printed postage permit box top-right of the address area:

```markdown
:::: {.side .back .mailer}
![](prism:logo){.logo}

## Join us on *June 12*

Two sentences.

- **When** 6–8 pm
- **Where** 100 Harbor Way

::: indicia
Presorted First-Class Mail · U.S. Postage Paid · Permit 000
:::
::::
```

Landscape is the usual mailer. Confirm the indicia text, address area and postage with the person's mail house before printing; never invent a permit number.

## Fitting

A side holds far less than a page: about 60–90 words on a standard side, 40–60 beside a cover photo, 25–40 on a mailer back, plus one component. The build prints `[sheet] 5x7 front: content runs ... past the bottom edge` (or `back`, `back column 2`) for any side that overflows; fix those first, then check the preview. Cut words first, then drop a component or image; never shrink type.

## Checks

- Two pages at most; one side is fine.
- At most one dark side.
- Fictional label or placeholder note on the back when figures or images are illustrative.
- QR codes on a card: `.small` is the smallest that prints reliably (0.85 in); keep the default size on a standard back.
- `bleed: true` when the person says it goes to a commercial printer; grounds and cover photos run into the bleed, text keeps its margin.
- `\ ` between the last two words of a title that would end on one word.
