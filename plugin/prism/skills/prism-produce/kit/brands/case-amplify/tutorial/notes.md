cedar hollow call notes (case study)

(ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE for the Prism tutorial, Cedar Hollow Family Services is a fictional agency, every person quote and figure below is invented)

call w/ program director + one of the team supervisors, typed up after

AGENCY
- Cedar Hollow Family Services — mid sized family services agency, 3 rural counties
-  38 caseworkers / 4 supervisors,  approx 1150 active families
- home visits are the core of the work. most caseworkers drive 40+ min between visits!!

## problem before
Caseworkers spent about 2.6 hrs a day writing case notes (their own time study, january). only 46 percent of visit notes were finished within 24 hours of the visit. backlog of notes averaged 9 days at the end of each month — worst right before audits

Director: "People were writing notes in their cars, at lunch, at their kitchen tables at 10 pm."

supervisors spent friday afternoons chasing late notes instead of coaching

What they did -

* rolled out Case Amplify over four months, starting Feburary
* month 1 = one pilot team of 8 caseworkers, month 2 two more teams, month 3 everyone
* after a visit the caseworker dictates what happend on their phone and CARA drafts the note in the agencys own template
* caseworker reads the draft, corrects anything it got wrong or left out, and approves it. nothing is saved untill they do
* CARA approves the routine notes automatically, so caseworkers only look at the tricky ones
*supervisors see a review queue of approved notes instead of a list of missing ones
* 2 hours training per team + a "note buddy" on each team for the first month
* no new staff hired during the rollout

Results (after 4 months, May, same time study)

by month (hrs/day on notes, then % done within 24 hrs):
jan (before) 2.6 hrs, 46%
feb 2.4 / 58%
march - 2.0 hrs a day, 71 %
april 1.7, 82%
may 1.5hrs 88%

- time on case notes down 41% (2.6 hrs to 1.5 hrs a day per caseworker)
- notes done w/in 24 hours 46% → 88%
- month end backlog 9 days to 2 days
- home visits per caseworker per week: 7.2 to 8.6
- supervisor said turnover is down too
- director said staff call it "the best tool the agency has ever bought"
