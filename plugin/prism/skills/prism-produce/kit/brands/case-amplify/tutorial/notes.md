# Cedar Hollow Family Services: call notes for the case study

ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE for the Prism tutorial. Cedar Hollow Family Services is a fictional agency. Every person, quote and figure below is invented for the example.

Call with the program director and a team supervisor, notes typed up afterwards.

## The agency

- Cedar Hollow Family Services, a mid-sized family services agency covering three rural counties
- 38 caseworkers, 4 supervisors, about 1,150 active families
- Home visits are the core of the work; most caseworkers drive 40+ minutes between visits

## The problem before

- Caseworkers spent about 2.6 hours a day writing case notes (their own time study, January)
- Only 46% of visit notes were finished within 24 hours of the visit
- The notes backlog averaged 9 days at the end of each month, worst before audits
- Director: "People were writing notes in their cars, at lunch, at their kitchen tables at 10 pm."
- Supervisors spent Friday afternoons chasing late notes instead of coaching

## What they did

- Rolled out Case Amplify over four months, starting February
- Month 1: one pilot team of 8 caseworkers; month 2: two more teams; month 3: everyone
- After a visit, the caseworker dictates what happened on their phone; CARA drafts the note in the agency's own template
- The caseworker reads the draft, corrects anything it got wrong or left out, and approves it. Nothing is saved until they do
- Supervisors see a review queue of approved notes instead of a list of missing ones
- Two hours of training per team; a "note buddy" on each team for the first month
- No new staff were hired during the rollout

## Results after four months (May, same time study)

| Month | Hours a day on notes | Notes done within 24 hours |
|---|---|---|
| January (before) | 2.6 | 46% |
| February | 2.4 | 58% |
| March | 2.0 | 71% |
| April | 1.7 | 82% |
| May | 1.5 | 88% |

- Time on case notes down 41% (2.6 hours to 1.5 hours a day per caseworker)
- Notes done within 24 hours: 46% to 88%
- Month-end backlog: 9 days to 2 days
- Home visits per caseworker per week: 7.2 to 8.6
- Supervisor said, roughly: "I think turnover is down too." No figures on turnover yet; don't use a number.
- Director said staff call it "the best tool the agency has ever bought". Not sure we can say that.
- Someone on the call said CARA "approves the routine notes automatically, so caseworkers only look at the tricky ones". That doesn't match how they described it above; check before it goes anywhere.
